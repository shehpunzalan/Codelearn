# How to Access Lesson References - Quick Guide

## 🎯 Quick Access

### Method 1: From Lesson Summary
1. Open any lesson in CodeLearn AI
2. Scroll to **Section 6: "Summary & Next Steps"**
3. Find **"References & Learning Resources"** section
4. View all 6 academic references with citations

### Method 2: From Video Tutorial
1. Click **"Video Tutorial"** button in any lesson
2. Scroll to **"About this Video"** section
3. Find **"Video Source"** citation box
4. View the full APA citation for the video

### Method 3: From Reading Content
1. Click **"Reading Content"** or **"Read More"** in any lesson
2. Navigate to **"Summary & Next Steps"** section
3. Find **"References & Learning Resources"** section
4. View all 6 academic references

## ✏️ Copying Citations

### To Copy a Citation:
1. Find the reference you need
2. Select the citation text
3. Right-click → Copy (or Ctrl+C / Cmd+C)
4. Paste into your document

All citations are in **APA 7th Edition** format, ready to use in:
- Research papers

- Study notes
